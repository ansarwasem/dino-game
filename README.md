Ek nihayat khoobsurat, rangeen (colorful), aur dilchasp Dinosaur running game jo bacho ke liye khas tor par design ki gayi hai taake wo khel kar booriat feel na karein! 🌈✨

---

## 🌟 Game Ke Zabardast Features (Khaas Batain):

1. **🎨 4 Cutest Dinosaur Characters (Apna Fav Dino Chuno):**
   - 🦖 **Rexy (Green Hero):** Classic taqatwar hero!
   - 🦕 **Spiky (Golden Stego):** Muskurata hua golden dost!
   - 🦩 **Pip (Pink Sweetie):** Cotton candy jaisa pyara!
   - 🐬 **Bubbles (Aqua Ocean):** Thanda thanda cool dinosaur!

2. **🌈 4 Changing Worlds & Themes (Har Score Par Nayi Duniya):**
   - ☀️ **Sunny Valley (0-250 score):** Neela aasmaan, phool aur suraj.
   - 🌅 **Sunset Magic (250-600 score):** Orange sunset aur rangeen badal.
   - 🍭 **Candy Dream (600-1100 score):** Meethi meethi strawberry candy jaisa jahan.
   - 🌌 **Starlight Space (1100+ score):** Chamakte sitaray aur chaand!

3. **⭐ Mazeedaar Goodies & Power-ups:**
   - ⭐ **Golden Stars:** +15 Points
   - 🍉 **Watermelon Slices:** +30 Points
   - 🌈 **Rainbow Shield:** 8 seconds ke liye super-shield! Dino chamakne lagta hai aur obstacles ko tor deta hai!
   - 🧲 **Super Magnet:** Saare fruits aur stars ko apni taraf kheench leta hai!
   - 💖 **Extra Heart:** Health bar wapis full karne ke liye!

4. **🎵 Real Web Audio Chiptune Sounds:**
   - Jump ki boing awaz, star collect karne ki sweet sound, aur background me chiptune music (jo mute/unmute bhi ho sakta hai).

5. **🎮 Controls (Asaan Tarika):**
   - **Keyboard:** `Spacebar` / `Up Arrow` (Jump), `Down Arrow` (Duck / Jhukna)
   - **Mobile / Tablet:** Screen ke neeche baray buttons hain: **JUMP ⬆️** aur **DUCK ⬇️**!
   - **Touch Tap:** Screen par tap karne se bhi Dino jump karta hai!

---

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment:
Ye game 100% static hai (No Node.js or backend required). Kisi bhi browser me `index.html` double-click karke chal sakti hai ya Vercel par 1-click me live ho sakti hai!
